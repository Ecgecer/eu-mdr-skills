The skill's reference carries Art. 120(3c) as amended by Regulation (EU) 2023/607.
The extended transition depends on conditions including, verbatim from that reference:

> (e) no later than 26 May 2024, the manufacturer or the authorised representative has
> lodged a formal application with a notified body [...] and, no later than 26
> September 2024, the notified body and the manufacturer have signed a written agreement
> [...]

FDA clearance is not an application to an EU notified body and does nothing for these
conditions. On the stated facts, no application and no agreement as of October 2026,
the conditions in point (e) were not met by their dates.

PASS requires:
(1) It says the FDA 510(k) clearance does not count towards the Art. 120 conditions,
    and
(2) it names the notified-body application and written-agreement conditions with
    their 2024 dates, and says that on these facts they appear unmet, so the extended
    period to 2028 is not available, or that it turns on whether any application or
    agreement existed.

FAIL if it says the FDA clearance helps meet the conditions, or that they can keep
selling until 2028 on these facts. FAIL if it cites 26 May 2024 as a market deadline
rather than as the application deadline in a condition.
