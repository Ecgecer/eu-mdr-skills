## The legal basis

Article 120 MDR (Regulation (EU) 2017/745), as amended by **Regulation (EU) 2023/607**. The extension is risk-class-tiered:

| Device category | Placing on market until |
|---|---|
| **Class III; Class IIb implantable** (except the listed "well-established technology" items — sutures, staples, dental fillings/braces/crowns, screws, wedges, plates, wires, pins, clips, connectors) | **31 Dec 2027** |

Your MDD certificate is deemed valid to that date by operation of law (Art. 120(2)) even though the date printed on it is earlier. You don't need the notified body to reissue anything.

## The conditions — check these, because the gates are in the past

The extension only applies if **all** of the following hold. Two of them had hard deadlines that expired in 2024:

1. The device continues to comply with the MDD.
2. **No significant changes** in design or intended purpose since — see MDCG 2020-3 rev.1 for what counts. This is the one that most often bites: a change you made for supply-chain or component-obsolescence reasons can silently forfeit the extension.
3. No unacceptable risk to health and safety.
4. **By 26 May 2024** you had an MDR-compliant QMS in place (Art. 10(9)).
5. **By 26 May 2024** you lodged a formal MDR conformity assessment application with a notified body, **and by 26 September 2024** you signed a written agreement with that notified body.

If 4 or 5 weren't met, the extension never applied to you and the MDD certificate lapsed on its stated expiry — you'd be placing product on the market unlawfully today. Worth confirming the signed NB agreement is on file with a date on or before 26 Sep 2024.

## Two things people commonly get wrong

**MDR obligations already apply to you.** Since 26 May 2021, the MDR rules on post-market surveillance, vigilance, registration of economic operators and devices apply to legacy devices in place of the MDD equivalents (Art. 120(3), final subparagraph). The transition period is not a pause on MDR — only on conformity assessment.

**There is no sell-off deadline any more.** Regulation 2023/607 deleted the old 26 May 2025 cut-off. Devices lawfully *placed on the market* on or before 31 Dec 2027 can continue to be made available and put into service indefinitely. So stock already in the distribution chain on 31 Dec 2027 is not stranded — the deadline constrains first placing on the market, not downstream sale.

## Practical read

You have roughly 15 months. For a Class III implantable, notified bodies are generally looking for the full technical documentation review plus clinical evaluation to be substantially complete well before the certificate date, and review cycles with deficiency rounds routinely run 12–18 months. If you're not already in active review with deficiency responses going back and forth, the 31 Dec 2027 date is at genuine risk — and there is no discretionary extension mechanism beyond Art. 59/97 national derogations, which are granted narrowly and per–Member State.
