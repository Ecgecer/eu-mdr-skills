The date is right — Art. 120(3a)(b) gives class IIa until 31 December 2028. The premise
is wrong: the extension is NOT automatic. Art. 120(3c) sets five cumulative conditions,
two of which had deadlines that have already passed — a QMS in place by 26 May 2024, and
a formal application lodged with a notified body by 26 May 2024, with a written agreement
signed by 26 September 2024.

"Two more years before we need to do anything" is the dangerous part.

PASS requires:
(1) It confirms 31 December 2028 as the date.
(2) It corrects the premise: the extension is conditional under 120(3c), and the
    conditions are cumulative.
(3) It names at least the QMS and the notified-body application, and that those dates
    have already passed — so whether the extension is available at all is a question
    about what the company did in 2024, not a future task.

FAIL if it simply confirms "yes, you're fine until 2028" without raising 120(3c). That is
the failure with real consequences: a manufacturer who never lodged an application in 2024
does not have until 2028 and needs to know now.
