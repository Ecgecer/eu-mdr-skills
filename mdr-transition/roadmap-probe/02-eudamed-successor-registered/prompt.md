Article 120(3). The MDR version of the same device was certified by our notified body
in February 2026 and is now on the market too; we registered that MDR device in
EUDAMED in June 2026. Both versions will be sold side by side until the legacy stock
runs out next year.

Do we also have to register the legacy version in EUDAMED before the November
deadline? Our team assumed yes, since it's still being placed on the market.
