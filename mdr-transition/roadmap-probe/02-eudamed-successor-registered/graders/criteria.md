MDR Art. 123(3)(e)(ii), as replaced by Regulation (EU) 2024/1860, verbatim (retrieved
from EUR-Lex 2026-10-18). The registration duty extends to:

> (ii) devices, other than custom-made devices, placed on the market pursuant to
> Article 120(3), (3a) or (3b), **unless the device, for which the manufacturer has
> undertaken a conformity assessment in accordance with Article 52, is already
> registered in Eudamed**;

The legacy device is still being placed on the market after 28 May 2026, so the
condition in the chapeau is met. The exception in bold is the point of the case: the
MDR-certified version of the device is already registered.

PASS requires the response to identify the exception for legacy devices whose
MDR-certified counterpart is already registered in EUDAMED, and apply it to these facts
as the likely answer (no separate legacy registration required), or as the deciding
question if it flags uncertainty about whether the two count as "the device" for this
purpose. Recommending confirmation against Commission or MDCG Q&A PASSES.

FAIL if it confirms the team's assumption (both must be registered) without addressing
the exception. FAIL if it says the legacy device must be registered "to be safe" while
never mentioning that the Regulation provides an exception.
