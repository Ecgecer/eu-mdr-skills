Two parts of the plan you described don't match how the MDR works. Fixing them first makes the rest simpler.

## 1. You don't upload your certificates. Your notified body does.

Under **MDR Article 56(5)**, the notified body enters the certificate information into the EUDAMED certificates module. That covers:
- certificates it issues
- amendments and supplements
- suspensions, reinstatements, withdrawals and refusals
- any restrictions it imposes

A manufacturer has no upload function for certificates. The "certificate uploads due" deadline you read about is the notified body's job.

## 2. You probably don't need an SSCP for these devices

**Article 32** requires a Summary of Safety and Clinical Performance only for **implantable devices and Class III devices**. A Class IIb non-implantable infusion pump is neither, so there's normally no SSCP to upload. Check this against your technical documentation and with your notified body. If a device has been classified as Class III, or has an implantable part, the answer changes.

## What *is* your job in EUDAMED

Once the relevant modules are mandatory, the manufacturer's steps are:

1. **Actor registration (Art. 31).** Register your company and get a Single Registration Number (SRN), if you don't have one yet. Your notified body needs the SRN to link certificates to you.
2. **UDI/device registration (Art. 29).** Register the Basic UDI-DI and the UDI-DIs for each pump model before placing it on the market. For a Class IIb device, the record points to the relevant notified body certificate.
3. **Check the link to your certificates.** Once the notified body has entered your certificates, confirm that each Basic UDI-DI is linked to the right certificate number and that the details match your records: scope, models, expiry date.
4. **Vigilance and post-market surveillance** come with later modules. For Class IIb, the PSUR goes to your notified body under Art. 86. It isn't uploaded to EUDAMED the way it is for Class III and implantable devices.

## Which certificates are in scope

Your notified body should register **every MDR certificate covering the three pumps that is still valid**. That includes those issued in 2023–2025 before EUDAMED became mandatory, and typically means:
- QMS certificates (Annex IX Chapter I)
- technical documentation assessment certificates (Annex IX Chapter II), or Annex XI certificates if that was your route
- every supplement or amendment issued since

If you still sell any **legacy MDD versions** of these pumps under Article 120, raise those certificates with your notified body as well. Legacy devices have their own registration rules.

## About the "May 2027" date

Regulation (EU) 2024/1860 changed Article 123 so EUDAMED becomes mandatory module by module:
- Each module becomes mandatory **6 months after** the Commission publishes a notice in the Official Journal confirming it works.
- Data about devices and certificates that existed before that date gets a longer transition period on top of the 6 months.

As far as I know, the notice for the first modules (actors, UDI/devices, notified bodies & certificates, market surveillance) came out in late 2025. That put the mandatory date around mid-2026, with transition deadlines for existing data running into 2027. A May 2027 date could plausibly be one of those deadlines. I'm not sure of the exact dates, though, so check them on the Commission's EUDAMED page and the Official Journal notice before you plan around them.
