# Item e01673417d

## The user's question

We have two legacy products that we are still placing on the EU market: a Class IIa
medical device under an MDD certificate, and an IVD under an IVDD certificate. Neither
has an MDR or IVDR successor registered yet.

What is the last day by which each must be registered in EUDAMED, and what is each
deadline counted from? I need the legal basis for both, not just a date.

## Grading criteria

The two deadlines are set by different provisions and counted from different anchors.
Both verbatim from Regulation (EU) 2024/1860 (retrieved from EUR-Lex 2026-10-18).

MDR Art. 123(3)(e):
> no later than **12 months from the date of publication of the notice** referred to in
> Article 34(3) [...]

IVDR Art. 113(3)(fa):
> no later than **6 months from the date set out in point (f), first subparagraph**, of
> this paragraph [...]

Point (f) is the date the electronic-system obligations apply: 6 months from
publication of the notice. The notice is Commission Decision (EU) 2025/2371, OJ
27.11.2025. So the MDR device is due 12 months from 27 November 2025, and the IVD 6
months from the mandatory-use date (28 May 2026). Both land in late November 2026.

PASS requires:
(1) Both deadlines placed in late November 2026, and
(2) separate legal bases named: MDR Art. 123 for the device and IVDR Art. 113 for the
    IVD (point letters not required), with the anchor for each stated or the difference
    acknowledged.

Do not fail on the day of the month. Under Regulation (EEC, Euratom) No 1182/71, 12
months from publication on 27 November 2025 ends at the close of 27 November 2026, and
the IVD date sits a day either side of that depending on how the anchor is read. Record
in the reason which days the response gave; that observation is part of the
measurement.

FAIL if it gives one date for both without distinguishing MDR and IVDR bases. FAIL if
either deadline is placed in 2027 or later, or is reasoned from the superseded rule
(registration due once all EUDAMED modules are functional, or within 18 months). FAIL if
it substitutes an IVDR Art. 110 transition date (certificate validity or market
deadline) for the registration deadline.

## The response to grade

**Short answer:** both products have the same deadline, about **28 May 2027**. In each case it is counted from the date EUDAMED registration became mandatory, about **28 May 2026**. It is **not** counted from your certificate dates, from when the product was first placed on the market, or from when the legacy transition period ends.

I had no source documents to check against here. The legal structure below I'm confident about. The specific notice reference and the dates come from memory, so check them in the Official Journal (EUR-Lex) before relying on them.

## Legal basis

**Amending act:** Regulation (EU) 2024/1860, which changed both the MDR and the IVDR to allow EUDAMED to go live module by module.

| | Class IIa legacy device (MDD) | Legacy IVD (IVDD) |
|---|---|---|
| Registration duty | MDR Art. 29 (UDI/device registration) and Art. 31 (actor registration) | IVDR Art. 26 (UDI/device registration) and Art. 28 (actor registration) |
| Transitional rule | **MDR Art. 123(3)(e)**, as amended by 2024/1860 | **IVDR Art. 113(3)(f)**, as amended by 2024/1860 |
| Start of the clock | 6 months after the Commission publishes its notice under **MDR Art. 34(3)** that the module works | 6 months after the notice under **IVDR Art. 34(3)** |
| Legacy device deadline | 12 months after that start date | 12 months after that start date |

**How the clock works:**
1. **The Commission notice.** The Commission confirms in the Official Journal that the relevant EUDAMED modules work. As I recall, this was **Commission Decision (EU) 2025/2371, published around 27 November 2025**. It covered four modules: actor registration, UDI/device registration, notified bodies and certificates, and market surveillance.
2. **Registration becomes mandatory.** The registration obligations apply from 6 months after that publication, so about **28 May 2026**.
3. **Grace period for legacy devices.** A legacy device that you keep placing on the market after that date must be registered **within 12 months of it**, so by about **28 May 2027**.

The one-day uncertainty (27 or 28 May 2027) depends on how the Official Journal date is counted under Regulation 1182/71 on time limits. Plan for the earlier date.

## Conditions that matter for you
- **The duty applies only while you keep selling.** It covers legacy devices still placed on the market after the mandatory date. You are, so it applies to both products.
- **An MDR or IVDR successor removes the duty.** You don't have to register the legacy device if a corresponding MDR or IVDR device is registered before the deadline. Neither of yours is, so this doesn't help you unless one is registered before May 2027.
- **Actor registration comes first.** You need an SRN (single registration number) from actor registration before you can register devices. If you don't have one yet, that is overdue now.
- **The EUDAMED deadline is separate from the transition period.** The period during which you may keep selling comes from different provisions:
  - MDD device: MDR Art. 120(3), as amended by Regulation 2023/607. For Class IIa that runs to 31 Dec 2028, subject to its conditions.
  - IVDD device: IVDR Art. 110(3), as amended by Regulations 2022/112 and 2024/1860.
  
  Being within the transition period doesn't postpone EUDAMED registration.

Since today is 19 October 2026, you have about seven months left. Before you fix internal dates, confirm three things on EUR-Lex: the notice reference, its publication date, and the current text of MDR Art. 123(3)(e) and IVDR Art. 113(3)(f).
