# Item e5115995ed

## The user's question

We have two legacy products that we are still placing on the EU market: a Class IIa
medical device under an MDD certificate, and an IVD under an IVDD certificate. Neither
has an MDR or IVDR successor registered yet.

What is the last day by which each must be registered in EUDAMED, and what is each
deadline counted from? I need the legal basis for both, not just a date.

## Grading criteria

The two deadlines are set by different provisions and counted from different anchors.
Both verbatim from Regulation (EU) 2024/1860 (retrieved from EUR-Lex 2026-10-18).

MDR Art. 123(3)(e):
> no later than **12 months from the date of publication of the notice** referred to in
> Article 34(3) [...]

IVDR Art. 113(3)(fa):
> no later than **6 months from the date set out in point (f), first subparagraph**, of
> this paragraph [...]

Point (f) is the date the electronic-system obligations apply: 6 months from
publication of the notice. The notice is Commission Decision (EU) 2025/2371, OJ
27.11.2025. So the MDR device is due 12 months from 27 November 2025, and the IVD 6
months from the mandatory-use date (28 May 2026). Both land in late November 2026.

PASS requires:
(1) Both deadlines placed in late November 2026, and
(2) separate legal bases named: MDR Art. 123 for the device and IVDR Art. 113 for the
    IVD (point letters not required), with the anchor for each stated or the difference
    acknowledged.

Do not fail on the day of the month. Under Regulation (EEC, Euratom) No 1182/71, 12
months from publication on 27 November 2025 ends at the close of 27 November 2026, and
the IVD date sits a day either side of that depending on how the anchor is read. Record
in the reason which days the response gave; that observation is part of the
measurement.

FAIL if it gives one date for both without distinguishing MDR and IVDR bases. FAIL if
either deadline is placed in 2027 or later, or is reasoned from the superseded rule
(registration due once all EUDAMED modules are functional, or within 18 months). FAIL if
it substitutes an IVDR Art. 110 transition date (certificate validity or market
deadline) for the registration deadline.

## The response to grade

# EUDAMED registration deadlines for MDD and IVDD legacy devices

**Short answer:** Both deadlines are counted from the same date and run for the same length. Each product must be registered within 12 months of the date EUDAMED registration became mandatory. That date is 6 months after the Commission published its notice that the modules work. On my reading this puts both deadlines at about 27/28 May 2027.

I had no access to EUR-Lex in this session, so all of this comes from memory. Check the citations against the consolidated texts before you rely on them.

## Legal basis

**Regulation (EU) 2024/1860** amended the transitional provisions so that EUDAMED could become mandatory one module at a time, instead of waiting until the whole system was finished:

- **MDR:** Article 123(3)(d)–(e) of Regulation (EU) 2017/745, as amended.
- **IVDR:** Article 113(3)(f)–(g) of Regulation (EU) 2017/746, as amended. The paragraph lettering differs from the MDR, but the mechanism is the same.

## What each deadline is counted from

1. **The trigger.** The Commission publishes a notice in the Official Journal that a module is fully functional (MDR Art. 34(3), applied through Art. 123(3)(d)). For the first four modules, including actor registration, UDI/device registration, and notified bodies and certificates, this was **Commission Decision (EU) 2025/2371, published 27 November 2025**.
2. **The date of application.** Registration obligations apply from **6 months after that publication**, which is **late May 2026** (the Commission has said 28 May 2026).
3. **The legacy device deadline.** Devices that are still placed on the market under Art. 120 MDR / Art. 110 IVDR must be registered **within 12 months of the date in step 2**. That gives roughly **27/28 May 2027** for both of your products.

   The obligation ends if an MDR or IVDR "successor" device is registered before the deadline. In that case the legacy device does not have to be registered.

   For comparison, MDR and IVDR devices already on the market before the date of application have a shorter window of **6 months**, which ended around 28 November 2026. That one does not apply to your two products.

## Points to check

- **Exact day.** Read the "six months after publication" rule together with Regulation 1182/71 on calculating time periods, and check the Commission's published date. It is 27 or 28 May depending on how the period is counted.
- **Letter numbering.** The point letters in IVDR Art. 113(3) are the citation I'm least sure about. Confirm them in the consolidated text.
- **Proposed changes.** The Commission proposed a targeted revision of the MDR and IVDR in December 2025. If it has been adopted, or progressed, since then, it could change these provisions. Check its status before you treat May 2027 as final.
- **Prerequisites.** Before you can register either device you need an SRN (actor registration). Under the legacy rules you also register the MDD or IVDD certificate data, and the IVD needs a notified body certificate.

Since today is 19 October 2026, you have about seven months for both products. Registering early avoids risk around the exact day the deadline falls on.
