# Item c4fbb4cac4

## The user's question

We have two legacy products that we are still placing on the EU market: a Class IIa
medical device under an MDD certificate, and an IVD under an IVDD certificate. Neither
has an MDR or IVDR successor registered yet.

What is the last day by which each must be registered in EUDAMED, and what is each
deadline counted from? I need the legal basis for both, not just a date.

## Grading criteria

The two deadlines are set by different provisions and counted from different anchors.
Both verbatim from Regulation (EU) 2024/1860 (retrieved from EUR-Lex 2026-10-18).

MDR Art. 123(3)(e):
> no later than **12 months from the date of publication of the notice** referred to in
> Article 34(3) [...]

IVDR Art. 113(3)(fa):
> no later than **6 months from the date set out in point (f), first subparagraph**, of
> this paragraph [...]

Point (f) is the date the electronic-system obligations apply: 6 months from
publication of the notice. The notice is Commission Decision (EU) 2025/2371, OJ
27.11.2025. So the MDR device is due 12 months from 27 November 2025, and the IVD 6
months from the mandatory-use date (28 May 2026). Both land in late November 2026.

PASS requires:
(1) Both deadlines placed in late November 2026, and
(2) separate legal bases named: MDR Art. 123 for the device and IVDR Art. 113 for the
    IVD (point letters not required), with the anchor for each stated or the difference
    acknowledged.

Do not fail on the day of the month. Under Regulation (EEC, Euratom) No 1182/71, 12
months from publication on 27 November 2025 ends at the close of 27 November 2026, and
the IVD date sits a day either side of that depending on how the anchor is read. Record
in the reason which days the response gave; that observation is part of the
measurement.

FAIL if it gives one date for both without distinguishing MDR and IVDR bases. FAIL if
either deadline is placed in 2027 or later, or is reasoned from the superseded rule
(registration due once all EUDAMED modules are functional, or within 18 months). FAIL if
it substitutes an IVDR Art. 110 transition date (certificate validity or market
deadline) for the registration deadline.

## The response to grade

Both deadlines run on the same clock. Each legacy product must be registered **no later than 12 months after the date EUDAMED's device-registration (UDI/Device) module became mandatory.** That mandatory date is itself 6 months after the Commission published its notice in the Official Journal saying the module is functional. The deadline is not counted from your certificate dates or from the notice publication itself.

I'm answering from memory of the legal texts; I had no sources to check against here. Check the point letters and the dates against the consolidated texts on EUR-Lex before relying on them.

## Medical device under an MDD certificate (MDR legacy device)

- **Legal basis:** Regulation (EU) 2024/1860 amended Article 123(3) of the MDR (Regulation (EU) 2017/745). The new point (d) makes EUDAMED obligations apply module by module, starting 6 months after the Commission publishes its notice under Article 34(3) that the module is functional. The new point (e) sets the catch-up period for devices already on the market. Legacy devices placed on the market under Article 120(3) must be registered within 12 months of that point (d) date.
- **Counted from:** the point (d) mandatory-use date of the device-registration module.
- **Exception:** you don't have to register the legacy device if you register its MDR successor ("corresponding device") before the deadline.

## IVD under an IVDD certificate (IVDR legacy device)

- **Legal basis:** Regulation (EU) 2024/1860 made the same change to Article 113(3) of the IVDR (Regulation (EU) 2017/746) for devices placed on the market under Article 110(3). It also extended the IVDR legacy transition periods in that same article.
- **Counted from:** the same thing, i.e. 12 months after the mandatory-use date of the device-registration module.
- **Exception:** the same one applies if the IVDR successor is registered first.

## Calendar dates

As I recall, the Commission's functionality notice for the first EUDAMED modules was published in late November 2025. That would make registration mandatory from about **28 May 2026**, which puts the legacy deadline for both products at about **28 May 2027**. ## Two related checks

1. **Do the legacy transitions still cover these products?** The registration deadline only matters while you're still allowed to place the device on the market. The MDD Class IIa device relies on MDR Article 120(3a)–(3c), as amended by Regulation (EU) 2023/607: up to 31 Dec 2028, provided you met the conditions, including a QMS by 26 May 2024 and a notified-body application and signed agreement by 26 May/26 Sept 2024. The IVDD device relies on IVDR Article 110(3), as amended by Regulation 2024/1860: 31 Dec 2027, 2028 or 2029, depending on its IVDR class. If either transition has lapsed, the registration question no longer applies to that product.
2. **Actor registration (SRN)** is a separate obligation, and it already applies from the module's mandatory date. You need an SRN before you can register any device.
