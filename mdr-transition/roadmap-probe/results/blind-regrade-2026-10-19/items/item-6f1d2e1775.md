# Item 6f1d2e1775

## The user's question

We have two legacy products that we are still placing on the EU market: a Class IIa
medical device under an MDD certificate, and an IVD under an IVDD certificate. Neither
has an MDR or IVDR successor registered yet.

What is the last day by which each must be registered in EUDAMED, and what is each
deadline counted from? I need the legal basis for both, not just a date.

## Grading criteria

The two deadlines are set by different provisions and counted from different anchors.
Both verbatim from Regulation (EU) 2024/1860 (retrieved from EUR-Lex 2026-10-18).

MDR Art. 123(3)(e):
> no later than **12 months from the date of publication of the notice** referred to in
> Article 34(3) [...]

IVDR Art. 113(3)(fa):
> no later than **6 months from the date set out in point (f), first subparagraph**, of
> this paragraph [...]

Point (f) is the date the electronic-system obligations apply: 6 months from
publication of the notice. The notice is Commission Decision (EU) 2025/2371, OJ
27.11.2025. So the MDR device is due 12 months from 27 November 2025, and the IVD 6
months from the mandatory-use date (28 May 2026). Both land in late November 2026.

PASS requires:
(1) Both deadlines placed in late November 2026, and
(2) separate legal bases named: MDR Art. 123 for the device and IVDR Art. 113 for the
    IVD (point letters not required), with the anchor for each stated or the difference
    acknowledged.

Do not fail on the day of the month. Under Regulation (EEC, Euratom) No 1182/71, 12
months from publication on 27 November 2025 ends at the close of 27 November 2026, and
the IVD date sits a day either side of that depending on how the anchor is read. Record
in the reason which days the response gave; that observation is part of the
measurement.

FAIL if it gives one date for both without distinguishing MDR and IVDR bases. FAIL if
either deadline is placed in 2027 or later, or is reasoned from the superseded rule
(registration due once all EUDAMED modules are functional, or within 18 months). FAIL if
it substitutes an IVDR Art. 110 transition date (certificate validity or market
deadline) for the registration deadline.

## The response to grade

# EUDAMED registration deadlines for your MDD and IVDD legacy devices

**Both deadlines fall on the same day, 28 May 2027, and both are counted from the same start date, 28 May 2026.** That start date is the day EUDAMED registration became mandatory. The deadlines are the same because both rules come from one amending regulation, Regulation (EU) 2024/1860, which wrote matching provisions into the MDR and the IVDR. The device class (IIa) makes no difference.

I couldn't check the Official Journal from here, so this is from memory. Confirm the dates and the exact article sub-paragraph letters on EUR-Lex before relying on them in a submission.

## The start date: 28 May 2026

- **MDR Art. 34(3):** the Commission publishes a notice in the Official Journal once EUDAMED modules have been audited and found functional.
- **MDR Art. 123(3)(d), as amended by Reg. 2024/1860:** registration obligations for a functional module apply **6 months after that notice is published.** This replaced the old rule that nothing applied until all of EUDAMED worked.
- **The notice:** Commission Decision (EU) 2025/2371 confirmed the first four modules work (actor registration, UDI/device registration, notified bodies and certificates, market surveillance). It was published in the OJ on **27 November 2025**, so the obligations apply from **28 May 2026**.
- **IVDR:** Art. 113(3), as amended by the same regulation, relies on the same MDR Art. 34(3) notice. EUDAMED is one system shared by both regulations, so the IVDR start date is also 28 May 2026.

## 1. Class IIa device under an MDD certificate

- **Legal basis:** MDR Art. 123(3)(e), as amended by Reg. 2024/1860, read with Art. 120(3), which is what lets MDD devices stay on the market as "legacy devices".
- **Rule:** a legacy device placed on the market from the start date must be registered in EUDAMED **no later than 12 months after that date.**
- **Deadline:** **28 May 2027**, counted from 28 May 2026.
- **Exception:** you don't need to register the legacy device if its MDR successor is registered before that deadline.

## 2. IVD under an IVDD certificate

- **Legal basis:** the matching IVDR transitional provision in Art. 113(3), as amended by Reg. 2024/1860, read with Art. 110(3), which covers IVDD legacy devices during the extended IVDR transition.
- **Rule:** the same 12 months, counted from the date the obligations apply.
- **Deadline:** **28 May 2027**, counted from 28 May 2026.
- **Exception:** the same as above. An IVDR successor registered before the deadline removes the obligation.

## Points to check

1. **The 6-month window doesn't apply to you.** Devices that already have an MDR or IVDR certificate get only 6 months from the start date (to about 28 November 2026). Since you have no MDR or IVDR successors, your two products follow the 12-month rule.
2. **Your actor registration (SRN) must come first.** Device registration needs it, so if you don't have one yet, do that well ahead of the deadline.
3. **Legacy devices have no Basic UDI-DI.** EUDAMED assigns a "EUDAMED DI" or "EUDAMED ID" in its place. Collect the UDI-DI and certificate data early.
4. **The exact deadline day.** Counting "12 months after" under Regulation 1182/71 can move the last day by one. Plan to submit before 27 May 2027 so the day doesn't matter.
5. **The successor exception depends on timing.** If an MDR or IVDR certificate for either product might arrive before May 2027, registering the successor before the deadline means you won't have to register the legacy device at all.
