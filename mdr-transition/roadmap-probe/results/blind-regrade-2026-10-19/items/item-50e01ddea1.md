# Item 50e01ddea1

## The user's question

We have two legacy products that we are still placing on the EU market: a Class IIa
medical device under an MDD certificate, and an IVD under an IVDD certificate. Neither
has an MDR or IVDR successor registered yet.

What is the last day by which each must be registered in EUDAMED, and what is each
deadline counted from? I need the legal basis for both, not just a date.

## Grading criteria

The two deadlines are set by different provisions and counted from different anchors.
Both verbatim from Regulation (EU) 2024/1860 (retrieved from EUR-Lex 2026-10-18).

MDR Art. 123(3)(e):
> no later than **12 months from the date of publication of the notice** referred to in
> Article 34(3) [...]

IVDR Art. 113(3)(fa):
> no later than **6 months from the date set out in point (f), first subparagraph**, of
> this paragraph [...]

Point (f) is the date the electronic-system obligations apply: 6 months from
publication of the notice. The notice is Commission Decision (EU) 2025/2371, OJ
27.11.2025. So the MDR device is due 12 months from 27 November 2025, and the IVD 6
months from the mandatory-use date (28 May 2026). Both land in late November 2026.

PASS requires:
(1) Both deadlines placed in late November 2026, and
(2) separate legal bases named: MDR Art. 123 for the device and IVDR Art. 113 for the
    IVD (point letters not required), with the anchor for each stated or the difference
    acknowledged.

Do not fail on the day of the month. Under Regulation (EEC, Euratom) No 1182/71, 12
months from publication on 27 November 2025 ends at the close of 27 November 2026, and
the IVD date sits a day either side of that depending on how the anchor is read. Record
in the reason which days the response gave; that observation is part of the
measurement.

FAIL if it gives one date for both without distinguishing MDR and IVDR bases. FAIL if
either deadline is placed in 2027 or later, or is reasoned from the superseded rule
(registration due once all EUDAMED modules are functional, or within 18 months). FAIL if
it substitutes an IVDR Art. 110 transition date (certificate validity or market
deadline) for the registration deadline.

## The response to grade

# EUDAMED registration deadlines for your MDD and IVDD legacy devices

**Short answer:** Both products have the same deadline, worked out the same way. Each must be registered in EUDAMED **within 12 months of the date the EUDAMED registration obligations started to apply**, which was **28 May 2026**. That puts the last day at **about 27–28 May 2027**. Plan to have both registered by **27 May 2027** at the latest.

I'm answering from my knowledge of the legal texts. I couldn't check the Official Journal from here, so please confirm the citations below against the consolidated texts on EUR-Lex before relying on them.

## Legal basis

**1. The amending regulation.** Regulation (EU) 2024/1860 changed the transitional EUDAMED provisions in both regulations:
- **Medical devices:** MDR (EU) 2017/745, Article 123(3)(d)–(e)
- **IVDs:** IVDR (EU) 2017/746, Article 113(3)(f) and following

It dropped the old rule that EUDAMED was mandatory only once every module was working. Now each module becomes mandatory separately.

**2. When the obligations started.** An obligation linked to a module applies **6 months after the Commission publishes a notice in the Official Journal** confirming that the module works (MDR Art. 34(3); IVDR Art. 34 via Art. 113). For the first four modules (actor registration, UDI/device registration, notified bodies and certificates, market surveillance):
- The notice was published on 27 November 2025 (Commission Decision (EU) 2025/2371, if I recall the number correctly).
- The obligations therefore applied from **28 May 2026**.

**3. The legacy device rule.** If a legacy device is still being placed on the market after that date, it must be registered **within 12 months of the date the obligation started to apply**. This covers:
- MDD/AIMDD devices under MDR Art. 120(3)
- IVDD devices under IVDR Art. 110(3)

The obligation lapses if a corresponding MDR or IVDR device is registered in EUDAMED before the 12 months run out. Since neither of your products has a successor registered, the obligation applies to both.

## What the deadline is counted from

The 12 months run from the **date of application of the obligations (28 May 2026)**. They do **not** run from when the notice was published (27 November 2025). People often get this wrong in two ways:
- Counting from the publication date, which gives roughly November 2026.
- Using the old 18-month legacy-device period from the earlier version of MDR Art. 123(3)(e) and MDCG 2021-1, which was replaced by Regulation 2024/1860.

**On the exact last day:** under Regulation 1182/71 on time limits, a period of months counted from 28 May 2026 can be read as ending at the end of 28 May 2027. A stricter reading of "within 12 months" gives 27 May 2027. Treat **27 May 2027** as your internal deadline so either reading is covered.

## Practical points

- **Do both registrations now.** Registering a legacy device in EUDAMED requires a validated actor registration (SRN). Legacy device records are also usually entered without a full UDI-DI, using a EUDAMED-assigned ID instead. Both steps take time.
- **The deadline can change.** If an MDR or IVDR successor is registered before May 2027, the legacy registration obligation for that product falls away. Don't rely on that unless the successor's certification will definitely be finished in time.
- **Vigilance is a separate timeline.** Reporting incidents through the EUDAMED vigilance module is not mandatory yet. It will follow its own 6-month clock once the Commission publishes a notice for that module.
