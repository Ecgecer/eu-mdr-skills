# Item 7fa5e2cd26

## The user's question

We have two legacy products that we are still placing on the EU market: a Class IIa
medical device under an MDD certificate, and an IVD under an IVDD certificate. Neither
has an MDR or IVDR successor registered yet.

What is the last day by which each must be registered in EUDAMED, and what is each
deadline counted from? I need the legal basis for both, not just a date.

## Grading criteria

The two deadlines are set by different provisions and counted from different anchors.
Both verbatim from Regulation (EU) 2024/1860 (retrieved from EUR-Lex 2026-10-18).

MDR Art. 123(3)(e):
> no later than **12 months from the date of publication of the notice** referred to in
> Article 34(3) [...]

IVDR Art. 113(3)(fa):
> no later than **6 months from the date set out in point (f), first subparagraph**, of
> this paragraph [...]

Point (f) is the date the electronic-system obligations apply: 6 months from
publication of the notice. The notice is Commission Decision (EU) 2025/2371, OJ
27.11.2025. So the MDR device is due 12 months from 27 November 2025, and the IVD 6
months from the mandatory-use date (28 May 2026). Both land in late November 2026.

PASS requires:
(1) Both deadlines placed in late November 2026, and
(2) separate legal bases named: MDR Art. 123 for the device and IVDR Art. 113 for the
    IVD (point letters not required), with the anchor for each stated or the difference
    acknowledged.

Do not fail on the day of the month. Under Regulation (EEC, Euratom) No 1182/71, 12
months from publication on 27 November 2025 ends at the close of 27 November 2026, and
the IVD date sits a day either side of that depending on how the anchor is read. Record
in the reason which days the response gave; that observation is part of the
measurement.

FAIL if it gives one date for both without distinguishing MDR and IVDR bases. FAIL if
either deadline is placed in 2027 or later, or is reasoned from the superseded rule
(registration due once all EUDAMED modules are functional, or within 18 months). FAIL if
it substitutes an IVDR Art. 110 transition date (certificate validity or market
deadline) for the registration deadline.

## The legal basis (the same for both products)

**Regulation (EU) 2024/1860** changed both regulations so that EUDAMED is made mandatory one module at a time, instead of all at once when the whole system was ready.

- **MDR (your Class IIa MDD device):** Article 123(3)(d) and (e) of Regulation (EU) 2017/745, as amended by 2024/1860.
- **IVDR (your IVDD device):** Article 113(3) of Regulation (EU) 2017/746, as amended by 2024/1860. This mirrors the MDR provisions. Check the letter numbering in the consolidated text.

How the two clocks work:

1. **When the module becomes mandatory.** The duty to register devices in the UDI/device registration module applies from **6 months after the Commission publishes a notice in the OJ** saying the module works. That notice is under Article 34(3) MDR and Article 31(3) IVDR as amended.
2. **The legacy deadline.** Devices certified under the old Directives that are still being placed on the market on that date must be registered **within 12 months of the date the obligation applies**. Under the original MDR and IVDR text this period was 18 months, counted from full EUDAMED functionality. 2024/1860 shortened it and changed what it's counted from.
3. **When the duty falls away.** You don't have to register a legacy device if you've already registered a corresponding MDR or IVDR device before the deadline. Neither of yours has one, so the duty applies to both.

So the deadline is **not** counted from:
- when you placed the device on the market,
- the MDR or IVDR general date of application (26 May 2021 or 26 May 2022), or
- your certificate dates.

It runs **from the date the device-registration module becomes mandatory**, plus 12 months.

## Related points

- **Actor registration comes first.** You need a Single Registration Number (SRN) before you can register devices. That duty has no 12-month grace period; it applies from the mandatory date.
- **Your right to keep selling** these devices is a separate question from the registration deadline:
  - **MDD Class IIa:** Article 120(3) MDR as amended by Regulation (EU) 2023/607 runs to 31 December 2028, provided its conditions are met: an MDR quality management system, an MDR application lodged, and a signed notified body agreement.
  - **IVDD:** Article 110 IVDR as amended by 2024/1860 runs to 2027, 2028 or 2029 depending on the IVDR class, again with conditions.
  
  Losing transitional status would end the legacy registration question altogether.
