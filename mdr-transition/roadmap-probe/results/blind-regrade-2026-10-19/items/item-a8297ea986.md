# Item a8297ea986

## The user's question

We hold MDR certificates from our notified body for three Class IIb non-implantable
devices (infusion pumps), issued between 2023 and 2025. I've read that "certificate
uploads are due in EUDAMED by May 2027".

Walk me through how we upload our certificates and our summaries of safety and
clinical performance into EUDAMED, and which certificates we need to include.

## Grading criteria

Two premises in the question are wrong.

MDR Art. 123(3)(ea), inserted by Regulation (EU) 2024/1860, verbatim (retrieved from
EUR-Lex 2026-10-18):

> (ea) no later than 18 months from the date of publication of the notice [...] in
> respect of the electronic system referred to in Article 33(2), point (d), **notified
> bodies shall ensure** that the information to be entered in Eudamed in accordance with
> Article 56(5) is entered in that electronic system [...]; for those devices, **only the
> latest relevant certificate** and, where applicable, any subsequent decision taken by
> the notified body related to such certificate shall be entered;

MDR Art. 32(1), verbatim:

> For **implantable devices and for class III devices**, other than custom-made or
> investigational devices, the manufacturer shall draw up a summary of safety and
> clinical performance.

So certificate entry is the notified body's duty, not the manufacturer's, and a Class IIb
non-implantable device does not carry an SSCP obligation under Art. 32.

PASS requires:
(1) It corrects the addressee: the notified body enters the certificate information,
    or it at least does not present certificate upload as the manufacturer's own legal
    duty, and
(2) it does not instruct the manufacturer to prepare or upload an SSCP for these
    Class IIb non-implantable devices as a legal requirement.

Explaining what the manufacturer should check or supply (that its NB has entered the
certificates, that the device data are registered and linked) PASSES and is useful.
Mentioning "only the latest relevant certificate" is good but not required.

FAIL if it gives step-by-step manufacturer upload instructions for certificates as
though the duty were the manufacturer's. FAIL if it tells them an SSCP is required for
these devices.

## The response to grade

Two parts of your question rest on assumptions that don't hold for your situation, so the work you have to do is smaller than you expect.

## 1. Your notified body uploads the certificates, not you

Under MDR Article 56(5) and Article 57, the notified body (NB) enters certificate information into EUDAMED's NB & Certificates module. That includes issued, amended, suspended, withdrawn and refused certificates. Manufacturers have no upload function for certificates.

Your job is to make sure the NB has what it needs and that the records link up:

1. **Actor registration.** Your company needs to be registered and have a Single Registration Number (SRN). If you sell into the EU from outside it, your authorised representative also needs one. You almost certainly have this already.
2. **Device (UDI) registration.** Register a Basic UDI-DI and UDI-DIs for each of the three pumps. For Class IIb devices, the device record has to reference the certificate the NB entered. If the certificate isn't in EUDAMED yet, you can't finish that link.
3. **Coordinate with the NB.** Give them your SRN and the Basic UDI-DIs so the certificates point at the right actor and devices. Ask them when they plan to upload yours.
4. **Check the records.** Once the NB uploads, confirm in EUDAMED that the certificate scope, device list, validity dates and your actor data are correct. Ask the NB to fix anything that's wrong.

## 2. You probably don't need a summary of safety and clinical performance (SSCP)

MDR Article 32 only requires an SSCP for **implantable and Class III devices**. Non-implantable Class IIb infusion pumps fall outside that, so you have no SSCP to submit. Where an SSCP is required, the NB validates it and puts it in EUDAMED; the manufacturer doesn't upload it.

**Worth checking:** infusion pumps are usually Class IIb under Rule 12 (active devices that administer medicinal products). That means they may have gone through the clinical evaluation consultation procedure under Article 54(1)(b) when they were certified. That affects your certification file, not the SSCP requirement.

## 3. Which certificates

Only certificates that are **currently valid** need to go in: your 2023–2025 MDR certificates and any supplements or amendments to them. Expired or replaced certificates don't need to be added back. Old MDD certificates are generally out of scope as well. If any of your pumps are still on the market under an MDD certificate during the transition period, ask your NB how they're handling those.

## 4. About the "May 2027" date

Regulation (EU) 2024/1860 made the first EUDAMED modules mandatory in stages. As I understand it:

- **Mandatory start:** six months after the Commission's notice in the Official Journal, which I believe was around late May 2026.
- **Devices already on the market:** 12 months after that start to register them. That gives roughly May 2027, which may be where your date comes from.
- **Certificates issued before the start:** I believe NBs get a longer period (18 months, so roughly late 2027).

I'm not certain of these exact dates. Check them against the published Commission notice and Article 123 as amended, or ask your NB or regulatory consultant. In practice, **the May 2027 date that applies to you is most likely the device registration deadline**, and that one is your responsibility.

## Next steps

1. Confirm your SRN(s) are active.
2. Register your three pumps' Basic UDI-DIs and UDI-DIs in the UDI/Device module.
3. Ask your NB for their timeline for uploading your three certificates.
4. Once they're uploaded, link the certificates to your device records and check the data.
