# Item 7e68538d23

## The user's question

We have two legacy products that we are still placing on the EU market: a Class IIa
medical device under an MDD certificate, and an IVD under an IVDD certificate. Neither
has an MDR or IVDR successor registered yet.

What is the last day by which each must be registered in EUDAMED, and what is each
deadline counted from? I need the legal basis for both, not just a date.

## Grading criteria

The two deadlines are set by different provisions and counted from different anchors.
Both verbatim from Regulation (EU) 2024/1860 (retrieved from EUR-Lex 2026-10-18).

MDR Art. 123(3)(e):
> no later than **12 months from the date of publication of the notice** referred to in
> Article 34(3) [...]

IVDR Art. 113(3)(fa):
> no later than **6 months from the date set out in point (f), first subparagraph**, of
> this paragraph [...]

Point (f) is the date the electronic-system obligations apply: 6 months from
publication of the notice. The notice is Commission Decision (EU) 2025/2371, OJ
27.11.2025. So the MDR device is due 12 months from 27 November 2025, and the IVD 6
months from the mandatory-use date (28 May 2026). Both land in late November 2026.

PASS requires:
(1) Both deadlines placed in late November 2026, and
(2) separate legal bases named: MDR Art. 123 for the device and IVDR Art. 113 for the
    IVD (point letters not required), with the anchor for each stated or the difference
    acknowledged.

Do not fail on the day of the month. Under Regulation (EEC, Euratom) No 1182/71, 12
months from publication on 27 November 2025 ends at the close of 27 November 2026, and
the IVD date sits a day either side of that depending on how the anchor is read. Record
in the reason which days the response gave; that observation is part of the
measurement.

FAIL if it gives one date for both without distinguishing MDR and IVDR bases. FAIL if
either deadline is placed in 2027 or later, or is reasoned from the superseded rule
(registration due once all EUDAMED modules are functional, or within 18 months). FAIL if
it substitutes an IVDR Art. 110 transition date (certificate validity or market
deadline) for the registration deadline.

## The response to grade

I don't have the OJ texts available here, and nothing in your workspace covers this, so the answer below comes from memory. The legal structure is solid. Check the exact calendar date and the point lettering against the consolidated texts before you rely on them in a regulatory file.

## How the deadline is set (both products)

Regulation (EU) 2024/1860 (OJ L, 9 July 2024) amended the MDR and IVDR so EUDAMED becomes mandatory module by module. Before that, nothing was mandatory until the whole system was declared functional.

1. **Trigger:** the Commission publishes a notice in the OJ under **Article 34(3) MDR** confirming that a EUDAMED module works. That notice also triggers the IVDR, through the IVDR's cross-reference to the MDR system.
2. **Mandatory-use date:** the module's obligations apply **6 months after that notice is published**. See **Art. 123(3)(d) MDR** and **Art. 113(3)(f) IVDR**, both as amended by 2024/1860.
3. **Legacy-device registration:** devices still placed on the market under an MDD/AIMDD or IVDD certificate must be registered in the UDI/device registration module **within 12 months of the mandatory-use date in step 2**. This applies only if no MDR/IVDR device that replaces it has been registered first. The provisions are the transitional points added to **Art. 123(3) MDR** and **Art. 113(3) IVDR** by 2024/1860. Legacy registration rests on **Art. 120(3)–(4) MDR** and **Art. 110(3)–(4) IVDR**, read with **Art. 29 MDR** and **Art. 26 IVDR**.

**What the deadline is counted from:** for both devices, the 12 months run from the **mandatory-use date of the UDI/device registration module**. They do not run from the notice's publication date, and they do not run from the date each device was placed on the market. In total, that is about 18 months after the OJ notice.

## The calendar date

The Commission's functionality decision/notice for the first four modules was published in the OJ in **late November 2025**. Those modules are actor registration, UDI/device registration, notified bodies and certificates, and market surveillance. As I recall it:

- **Mandatory use:** about late May 2026
- **Last day to register legacy devices (both your Class IIa MDD device and your IVDD device):** about **late May 2027**

I'm not confident of the exact day. Take the OJ publication date of the notice, add 6 months to get the mandatory-use date, then add 12 months to get your last day. Check the result against the notice itself and the Commission's EUDAMED timeline page.

## Points that could change your answer

- **A successor device ends the legacy route.** If you register an MDR or IVDR device that replaces the legacy one, the legacy device no longer needs its own registration. The replacement device must be registered **before** it is placed on the market. The 12-month window doesn't apply to it.
- **Your certificates must still be valid.** Registration doesn't extend market access. The Class IIa MDD device depends on the conditions of Art. 120 MDR as amended by Regulation (EU) 2023/607. The IVDD device depends on Art. 110 IVDR as amended by Regulation (EU) 2024/1860, which sets staggered dates by risk class.
- **You must be registered as an actor first.** Actor registration (SRN) has been mandatory since the same mandatory-use date. You can't register a device without it.
