# Item 4f21c4d0dd

## The user's question

We have two legacy products that we are still placing on the EU market: a Class IIa
medical device under an MDD certificate, and an IVD under an IVDD certificate. Neither
has an MDR or IVDR successor registered yet.

What is the last day by which each must be registered in EUDAMED, and what is each
deadline counted from? I need the legal basis for both, not just a date.

## Grading criteria

The two deadlines are set by different provisions and counted from different anchors.
Both verbatim from Regulation (EU) 2024/1860 (retrieved from EUR-Lex 2026-10-18).

MDR Art. 123(3)(e):
> no later than **12 months from the date of publication of the notice** referred to in
> Article 34(3) [...]

IVDR Art. 113(3)(fa):
> no later than **6 months from the date set out in point (f), first subparagraph**, of
> this paragraph [...]

Point (f) is the date the electronic-system obligations apply: 6 months from
publication of the notice. The notice is Commission Decision (EU) 2025/2371, OJ
27.11.2025. So the MDR device is due 12 months from 27 November 2025, and the IVD 6
months from the mandatory-use date (28 May 2026). Both land in late November 2026.

PASS requires:
(1) Both deadlines placed in late November 2026, and
(2) separate legal bases named: MDR Art. 123 for the device and IVDR Art. 113 for the
    IVD (point letters not required), with the anchor for each stated or the difference
    acknowledged.

Do not fail on the day of the month. Under Regulation (EEC, Euratom) No 1182/71, 12
months from publication on 27 November 2025 ends at the close of 27 November 2026, and
the IVD date sits a day either side of that depending on how the anchor is read. Record
in the reason which days the response gave; that observation is part of the
measurement.

FAIL if it gives one date for both without distinguishing MDR and IVDR bases. FAIL if
either deadline is placed in 2027 or later, or is reasoned from the superseded rule
(registration due once all EUDAMED modules are functional, or within 18 months). FAIL if
it substitutes an IVDR Art. 110 transition date (certificate validity or market
deadline) for the registration deadline.

## The response to grade

The deadlines are the same for both products: **28 May 2027**. Both are counted **12 months from 28 May 2026**, the date EUDAMED registration became mandatory. That start date is itself counted from when the Commission published its EUDAMED notice in the Official Journal. I don't have web access in this session, so this is from memory. Check the exact wording and point numbering against the consolidated texts on EUR-Lex before you rely on it.

## The rule that applies to both

**Regulation (EU) 2024/1860** changed the MDR and the IVDR so that EUDAMED could go live one module at a time.

- For each module, the obligations apply **6 months after the Commission publishes a notice in the Official Journal** confirming the module works. For the MDR this is the Art. 34(3) notice. The IVDR links to the same procedure.
- The Commission published its Decision on the first four modules on **27 November 2025**. Those modules are actor registration, UDI/device registration, notified bodies and certificates, and market surveillance.
- Use of those modules became **mandatory from 28 May 2026**.

## Class IIa device under an MDD certificate (MDR legacy device)

- **Legal basis:** MDR Art. 123(3)(d)–(e), as amended by Art. 1 of Reg. 2024/1860. Read it with Art. 120(3), which covers legacy devices, and Art. 29, which covers device registration.
- **Rule:** A legacy device that is still being placed on the market must be registered in EUDAMED **within 12 months of the date the obligation applies**. The exception is where a successor device under the MDR has already been registered, in which case that registration counts.
- **Counted from:** 28 May 2026, not the November 2025 publication date.
- **Deadline:** **28 May 2027**

## IVD under an IVDD certificate (IVDR legacy device)

- **Legal basis:** IVDR Art. 113(3), the EUDAMED point (originally (f)), as amended by Art. 2 of Reg. 2024/1860. Read it with Art. 110(3) on legacy devices and Art. 26 on registration.
- **Rule and starting point:** Same 12-month window, counted from the same 28 May 2026 date. Reg. 2024/1860 made the IVDR mirror the MDR here.
- **Deadline:** **28 May 2027**

## Points to check

1. **Point numbering:** Reg. 2024/1860 replaced and inserted some sub-points in Art. 123(3) MDR and Art. 113(3) IVDR. Cite whatever letters the consolidated version on EUR-Lex uses.
2. **Day counting:** "Six months after publication" and "12 months from" are counted under Regulation 1182/71 on time limits. The Commission's own communications give 28 May 2026 as the start date, and 28 May 2027 follows from that. If you want to be safe, plan to finish by 27 May 2027.
3. **Different 6-month window:** There is a separate, shorter window for devices placed on the market *before* 28 May 2026. It applies to devices that already hold MDR or IVDR certificates, not to legacy devices. Don't mix the two up.
4. **If you register a successor:** Registering an MDR or IVDR successor before the deadline replaces the need to register the legacy device.
5. **Prerequisite:** You need an SRN (actor registration) before you can register any device. That obligation has applied since 28 May 2026, so if you don't have one yet, it's already overdue.
