We're preparing the clinical evaluation for a Class IIa, non-implantable, non-active
wound-irrigation device. We want to claim equivalence to a competitor's CE-marked
device using its IFU, published bench data, and three published clinical studies.

Our RA consultant says MDR Article 61(5) makes equivalence to another manufacturer's
device impossible without a contract giving us full access to their technical
documentation. Is that right for our device? And is the published material enough?
