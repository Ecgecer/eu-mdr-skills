Verbatim from Regulation (EU) 2017/745 (retrieved from EUR-Lex 2026-10-18).

Art. 61(4), first lines:
> In the case of **implantable devices and class III devices**, clinical
> investigations shall be performed, except if: [...]

Art. 61(5):
> A manufacturer of a device demonstrated to be equivalent to an already marketed
> device not manufactured by him, **may also rely on paragraph 4 in order not to perform
> a clinical investigation** provided that the following conditions are fulfilled in
> addition to what is required in that paragraph: — the two manufacturers have a
> contract in place that explicitly allows the manufacturer of the second device full
> access to the technical documentation on an ongoing basis, and [...]

Annex XIV, Part A, Section 3, last sentence:
> It shall be clearly demonstrated that manufacturers have **sufficient levels of
> access to the data** relating to devices with which they are claiming equivalence in
> order to justify their claims of equivalence.

The contract condition belongs to the Art. 61(4) regime for implantable and Class III
devices. It does not reach a non-implantable Class IIa device. But Annex XIV still
requires technical, biological and clinical equivalence, plus sufficient access to the
data, and an IFU, bench summaries and literature may not establish technical and
biological characteristics (materials, specifications).

PASS requires both:
(1) The Art. 61(5) contract requirement does not apply to this Class IIa
    non-implantable device; the consultant has over-applied it, and
(2) the response flags that Annex XIV still requires sufficient access to the data to
    demonstrate technical, biological and clinical equivalence, and that published
    material alone may not provide it.

Citing MDCG 2020-5 PASSES if it is identified as guidance rather than binding text.

FAIL if it confirms a contract is legally required for this device. FAIL if it says the
published material is sufficient without raising the access or characteristics
question.
