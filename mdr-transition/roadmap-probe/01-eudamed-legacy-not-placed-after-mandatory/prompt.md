It's October 2026. One of our products is a Class IIb device we were still selling
under its MDD certificate as a legacy device under Article 120(3). We stopped placing
it on the market on 31 March 2026: the last unit left our warehouse that day.
Distributors still have stock they are selling through.

Our consultant says every legacy device has to be registered in EUDAMED by
November 2026 or it is illegal to have it on the market, so we need to register this
one now and tell the distributors to stop selling until we do. Is that right?
