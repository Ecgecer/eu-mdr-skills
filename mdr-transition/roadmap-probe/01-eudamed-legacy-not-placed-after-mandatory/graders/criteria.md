The consultant's premise is "every legacy device". The provision is narrower.

MDR Art. 123(3)(e), as replaced by Regulation (EU) 2024/1860, verbatim (OJ L,
2024/1860, 9.7.2024, retrieved from EUR-Lex 2026-10-18):

> (e) no later than 12 months from the date of publication of the notice referred to
> in Article 34(3) in respect of the electronic system referred to in Article 33(2),
> points (a) and (b), manufacturers shall ensure that the information to be entered in
> Eudamed in accordance with Article 29 is entered in that electronic system, including
> regarding the following devices, **provided that those devices are also placed on the
> market from 6 months from the date of publication of that notice**: [...]
> (ii) devices, other than custom-made devices, placed on the market pursuant to
> Article 120(3), (3a) or (3b), unless [...]

The notice is Commission Decision (EU) 2025/2371, published 27.11.2025. Six months
from publication is the mandatory-use date, 28 May 2026. A legacy device whose last unit
was placed on the market on 31 March 2026 does not meet the condition in bold. Units a
distributor sells afterwards are being made available, not placed on the market: placing
on the market is the first making available.

PASS requires:
(1) It identifies that the legacy-device registration duty is conditioned on the
    device being placed on the market from the mandatory-use date (28 May 2026), or
    states that whether units were placed on the market after that date is the deciding
    fact, and
(2) it does not endorse the consultant's conclusion that this device must be registered
    and distributors must stop selling because it is unregistered.

Saying "confirm no unit was placed on the market after 28 May 2026", or recommending
the competent authority or Commission Q&A be checked, PASSES and is good practice.

FAIL if it confirms every legacy device must be registered regardless of when it was
last placed on the market. FAIL if it tells the manufacturer to stop distributors selling
on account of non-registration. FAIL if it reasons from the superseded rule (registration
only once all EUDAMED modules are functional, or an 18-month window).
