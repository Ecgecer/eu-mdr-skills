We are the only manufacturer of a neonatal ECMO cannula that is used in about forty
hospitals across nine EU Member States. We will discontinue it in nine months. We are
established in the Netherlands and have no authorised representative.

Under MDR Article 10a, who exactly do we have to inform, and by when? Do we have to
notify the competent authority of every Member State where it is used, plus the
Commission, ourselves?
