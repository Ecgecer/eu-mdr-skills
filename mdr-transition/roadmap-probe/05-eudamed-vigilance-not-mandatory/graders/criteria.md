"EUDAMED became mandatory" is true only of the electronic systems the notice covered.

Commission Decision (EU) 2025/2371 (OJ 27.11.2025), Article 1, verbatim (retrieved from
EUR-Lex 2026-10-18), confirms functionality of exactly four systems:

> (a) the electronic system on registration of economic operators [...];
> (b) the UDI database and the electronic system for registration of devices [...];
> (c) the electronic system on notified bodies and certificates [...];
> (d) the electronic system on market surveillance [...]

Vigilance and post-market surveillance, Art. 33(2)(f), is not among them. MDR Art.
123(3)(d), second subparagraph, as replaced by Regulation (EU) 2024/1860:

> Until the date of application of the provisions referred to in the first
> subparagraph of this point, the corresponding provisions of Directives 90/385/EEC and
> 93/42/EEC regarding information on vigilance reporting, clinical investigations,
> registration of devices and economic operators, and certificate notifications shall
> continue to apply;

PASS requires the response to decline the confirmation. It must say the 28 May 2026
mandatory date covers specific modules and that vigilance is not among them. It may
also say it is not established that vigilance reporting through EUDAMED is mandatory,
and that reporting continues under the existing arrangements until a separate notice
for that system takes effect.

A response that says a later Commission notice could have been published and should be
checked PASSES; that is correct discipline. A response that names the four modules
without the article numbers PASSES.

FAIL if it confirms EUDAMED vigilance is now the required route. FAIL if it writes SOP
text making EUDAMED the mandatory vigilance channel.
