We make Class I non-sterile wooden tongue depressors. Our timber supplier has closed,
and we expect a three-month gap in our supply starting in six weeks. Dozens of other
manufacturers sell equivalent tongue depressors across the EU, and our customers
already dual-source.

Our new compliance hire says MDR Article 10a required us to notify the competent
authority six months in advance, so we are already in breach. Are we?
