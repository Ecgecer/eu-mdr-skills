Standalone app, not connected to any device. Patients with chronic kidney disease
enter their weight and blood pressure once a day at home, and the app trends the
readings over weeks and shows the trend to the patient and their nephrologist at the
next appointment. There are no alarms and it is not used for any acute decision.
Intended purpose: "to record and trend patient-entered weight and blood pressure over
time for review during scheduled nephrology consultations."
