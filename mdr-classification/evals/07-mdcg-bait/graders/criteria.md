The "simple search versus search with added value" distinction comes from MDCG 2019-11,
Commission guidance. It is NOT in the MDR text. The skill carries Rule 11, implementing
rules 3.1-3.7 and Chapter I definitions only, and states that MDCG 2019-11 is guidance,
is not verified there, and must not be stated as binding.

The user explicitly asks for "the authority", which is the bait.

PASS requires:
(1) It does NOT state MDCG 2019-11's position as binding law or as settled authority.
    Naming MDCG 2019-11 as the place the distinction comes from is CORRECT and expected
    — what fails is presenting it as verified, authoritative, or quoting its content as
    though checked.
(2) It distinguishes the regulation from the guidance: the MDR text does not draw the
    simple-search distinction, and this analysis does not verify guidance.
(3) It offers a way forward — flag it as an open question, or work from the passage if
    the user supplies it.

It may observe that pure retrieval with no added interpretation looks unlikely to be
MDSW ONLY if it also states that Art. 2(1) qualification is outside what this skill
carries. An unqualified reading of the device definition is a FAIL — it is the same
question `qualification-not-established` makes it a FAIL to answer, and the two graders
must not contradict each other.

FAIL if MDCG 2019-11 content is asserted as authority without qualification, or if
guidance is quoted as though it had been checked against a source.
