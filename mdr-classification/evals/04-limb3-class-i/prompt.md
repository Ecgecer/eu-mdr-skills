What class is this under MDR Annex VIII?

Standalone software used in hospitals. It takes the finished, clinician-signed
radiology report and reformats it into the referring GP's preferred layout, then files
it to the GP's inbox. It does not analyse images, does not alter clinical content, and
adds nothing to the report. Qualification as a medical device is already established
and not in question. Intended purpose: "to reformat and route finalised radiology
reports to referring physicians."
