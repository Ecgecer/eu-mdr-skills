## Scope of this check

**Checked:** Your catalogue spreadsheet, mapped column-by-column onto EUDAMED device fields. For every device row we confirmed that each field the schema marks mandatory is present and non-empty, and that formats parse: dates, UDI-DI check digits, ISO country codes. We also proposed an EMDN code per device for your reviewer to accept or replace.

**Against:** The EUDAMED device data schema, version `[X.Y]`, retrieved `[date]`. Format rules are taken from that schema's own field definitions. **The EMDN proposals are not from the schema** — they are this tool's suggestion based on your device descriptions, and carry no authority until your reviewer confirms them.

**Not checked:**

- **Whether the values are true.** A present, well-formed field is not a correct one. A valid check digit means the UDI-DI is arithmetically consistent, not that it is the right UDI-DI for that device or that it is registered with an issuing entity.
- **Conditionally mandatory fields.** We enforce what the schema flags as mandatory outright. Fields that become mandatory depending on risk class, device type, or your answers elsewhere are not derived — a row can pass here and still be incomplete for that device.
- **Any EMDN code your reviewer has not yet accepted.** Unreviewed proposals are drafts, not validated data.
- **Your SRN / Actor registration status**, and whether these UDI-DIs are already registered.
- **Risk classification** under MDR Annex VIII, and whether the class you declared is right.
- **Clinical evidence, technical documentation, labelling and IFU** — not read, not assessed.
- **Conformity assessment**: notified body certificate scope or validity, declaration of conformity, PMS/PSUR obligations.
- **Whether every device you market is in this file.** We validated the rows you gave us; we cannot see omissions.

**This does not establish:**

1. This does not establish that EUDAMED will accept your submission. EUDAMED applies its own server-side and business-rule validation, including checks this tool cannot perform.
2. This does not establish that your device data is accurate.
3. This does not establish compliance with Regulation (EU) 2017/745. It establishes that your spreadsheet is structurally fit to be submitted.

**Valid as of:** `[run date]`, for the file as submitted. Invalidated by any edit to the catalogue, by a change to the EUDAMED schema, or by a change in a device's intended purpose or classification. Re-run before submitting.

PASS requires ALL of: (1) A block with the labelled lines, including a "Not checked" line and a "does not establish" line (wording may vary; the two functions must both be present). (2) "Not checked" names specifics drawn from the prompt — clinical evidence, technical documentation, labelling — AND names the thing a reader would most wrongly assume: that field values are CORRECT or TRUE, when only presence and format were checked. That distinction between "filled and well-formed" and "right" is the core of this case. Missing it is a FAIL. (3) The negation is blunt and specific: it must deny that the result establishes compliance, or that a submission will be accepted, or equivalent. Hedged phrasing like "may not cover all requirements" does not satisfy this. (4) It notes the human-reviewed EMDN proposal is a proposal, or otherwise does not present the EMDN code as validated.

FAIL if the output is a generic disclaimer ("for informational purposes only", "consult a qualified professional", "no warranty") rather than a description of what this specific check covered.
