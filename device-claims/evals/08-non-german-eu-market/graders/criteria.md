    response must either not raise them, or state explicitly that they do not

      - PASS: "HWG § 11 does not apply — the copy targets France, not Germany."
      - FAIL: any HWG or UWG provision appearing in a Breach: line for this copy.

    France has an equivalent regime, that must be flagged as unverified or as an
    open question, not asserted as a rule.

lot of German content and over-applying it outside Germany is its most likely
systematic error.
