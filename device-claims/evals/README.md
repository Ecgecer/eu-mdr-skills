## Correction, 2026-09-10

An earlier version of this file published **`puffery-restraint` as 1.00 / 0.67** and
**`hwg3a-arzneimittel-only` as 1.00 / 0.33**, and asserted "**All ten score 1.00 with the
skill.**"

None of that came from the data. The harness printed only a mean delta for those runs,
and the arm values were **inferred backwards from the delta** to make the table
consistent. The stored runs say 0.33 / 0.00 and 0.67 / 0.00. The deltas were right by
luck; the arms were invented, and two cases do not score 1.00.

That is why this table is generated now.

## Two cases score below 1.00 with the skill, and one of them is a grader defect

`puffery-restraint` at **0.33** is not the skill failing. The stored with-run that three
judges failed contains:

> **Claim:** "Surgeons love it."
> **Breach:** none
> **Also engaged:** UWG § 5(2) no. 1 — if this is presented as a factual finding
> **Not engaged:** HWG § 11(1) no. 11. § 11 applies only outside Fachkreise.

and, on limb (c):

> "I'm not going to infer one from 'surgical retractor' and flag the copy for omitting
> it; that reasoning would condemn every advertisement ever written."

Every `Breach:` line says none, § 11 is correctly excluded, and the (c) discipline is
executed exactly as `SKILL.md` teaches. The grader failed it because condition (1) reads
"No provision appears in a Breach: line" as a string test, and it is blind to
`Also engaged:` — the one mechanism the skill invents specifically for this situation,
and which **no grader in this repo mentions**.

`hwg3a-arzneimittel-only` at **0.67** needs the same audit and has not had it.
