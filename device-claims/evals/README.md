## Measured 2026-09-10, after two rounds of grader repair

These numbers are from graders corrected twice: first for being too loose (they would
have passed "testimonials are prohibited", which the reference file says in terms is
wrong), then for being too prescriptive (they demanded a literal `Breach: none` +
`Also engaged:` + `Call: Verify` triple and failed better answers that reached the same
place in prose).

Earlier published figures for this suite were wrong in three ways, invented arm values,
a stale grader quoting a prompt that had been rewritten, and the most favourable of
several runs. All in [`../../CORRECTIONS.md`](../../CORRECTIONS.md).

## Six cases at +1.00, and they are all one shape

`clean-copy-control`, `hwg11-item-scope`, `hwg11-wrong-audience`, `limb-c-omission`,
`non-german-eu-market` and `puffery-restraint`. With no reference material Claude, in
**3 of 3 runs each**:

- manufactured findings on copy that tracks its intended purpose and states its limits
- cited HWG § 11(1) Nr. 2 against a medical device. A provision that reaches devices
  only through nos. 7, 8, 9, 11 and 12
- applied a lay-audience rule to a gated professional audience
- asserted French advertising rules it cannot cite, having correctly ruled German
  law out of a French-market asset
- manufactured a finding on pure puffery

None of that is a knowledge failure. Every provision it cited is real and it described
them accurately. It applied them to products, markets and audiences they do not reach.

## Two cases diagnosed, then fixed and re-measured

Both sat at delta 0.00 with opposite causes, which is the argument for reading
transcripts rather than scores.

**`no-case-law-supplement`: 0.00 → +0.67. The grader was wrong.** The case scored 0.00 in
both arms, and the skill was doing exactly what it should. Two of three runs refused the
docket number outright, routed the evidentiary question to a Fachanwalt with three
specific questions, and did the statute work they could source. One applied the newest
rule explicitly, declining even to say there is no leading case, because that is itself an
unsourced claim about case law.

All three judges failed them. The grader said FAIL if a BGH holding or standard appeared
"AT ALL", so a response quoting the tempting wrong answer in order to disavow it tripped
the rule, and so did the skill's own mandatory Limits block, which states that UWG is
shaped by BGH case law this review does not assess. The grader now judges whether the
response **asserts** case law and names the disavowal as the strongest pass. Re-measured:
**0.67 / 0.00**.

**`uwg6-comparison`: 0.00 → +0.33. The skill was wrong.** On "Half the rework of what most
teams use today" the failing run reached the right analysis, no defined comparator, no
denominator a reader can reconstruct, and recorded `Breach: none on the material
supplied` with "Evidence needed: the study behind half". That sends the user after data
that cannot fix it: § 6(2) no. 2 asks what the **reader** can check from public
information, not what the advertiser holds. The skill framed § 6(2) as a checklist without
saying what failing it means; it now says failing no. 2 is a breach on the face of the
copy. Re-measured: **1.00 / 0.67**, the with-arm at full marks.

`no-case-law-supplement` still fails one run of three. Two of three is not a finished
case, and it is the only case here where the skill and the grader have each been wrong
once.

## There are no zeroes left here, and that is worth suspicion

Every one of these ten cases now measures a positive delta. On 2026-09-09 four of them
measured 0.00, and this file's argument has always been that publishing those is what
makes the rest believable.

Three things moved them, and only one is a win:

- `limb-c-omission` and `limb-d-intended-purpose-drift` moved because the **baseline got
  worse**, not because the skill got better. Their with-arms were already at or near 1.00.
  A delta that grows because the unaided model had a bad day is not an improvement in the
  skill, and at three runs an arm it is the most likely thing to be noise.
- `uwg6-comparison` moved because a real defect was found and fixed, with the with-arm
  going 0.67 to 1.00 against a steady baseline. That one is earned.
- `no-case-law-supplement` moved because its grader was wrong, not because behaviour
  changed.

**A suite where nothing measures zero has usually stopped being a fair test.** The
repo-wide figure is the one to trust, roughly half of all cases across the five suites
still measure 0.00, and this suite should be watched for having drifted toward cases that
flatter the skill. The honest next step here is a new case aimed at something Claude
already does well unaided, not another case in the shape that earns.
