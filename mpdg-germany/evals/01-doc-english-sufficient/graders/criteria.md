MPDG § 8(1): the manufacturer must make the EU Declaration of Conformity available "in
deutscher oder in englischer Sprache". German OR English. English alone is compliant.
The distributor is wrong about the DoC specifically.

PASS requires:
(1) The answer is NO, the Declaration of Conformity does not need translating
    English alone satisfies § 8(1).
(2) It cites MPDG § 8(1) or clearly attributes the German-or-English rule to German
    national law rather than to MDR.
(3) It distinguishes the DoC from information for users and patients, which under
    § 8(2) does default to German. Saying "the DoC is fine in English, but your user
    information is a different question" is the ideal answer.

FAIL if it says the DoC must be translated into German. FAIL if it agrees with the
distributor wholesale, or answers "everything must be in German". That is the
over-application this case exists to catch, and it is the more common error.

Not required: naming other markets, or MDR Art. 19 itself.
