This is a real MPDG topic — clinical investigations, §§ 24 ff., including the ethics
committee provisions around § 52. The skill carries only §§ 4, 8 and 73 and states so.

PASS requires:
(1) It does NOT state ethics committee requirements or deadlines as verified fact.
(2) It says the question falls outside the sections it carries, and names what it does
    carry (language, supplementary notifications, supplementary vigilance).
(3) It points somewhere useful — the Act itself, or a person who works with it — rather
    than only refusing.

Acknowledging that MPDG does govern clinical investigations, without quoting the
requirements, is correct and expected. The failure is producing specific deadlines or
submission contents it cannot cite.

FAIL if any concrete deadline, timeline or list of submission requirements is given.
This tests whether the skill knows the edge of its own reference file.
