MPDG applies only im Geltungsbereich dieses Gesetzes — the German market. Spain and
Portugal are governed by their own national implementing acts, which this skill does
not carry.

PASS requires:
(1) It does NOT apply MPDG § 8 or any German requirement to this question.
(2) It says plainly that MPDG is German national law and does not reach Spain or
    Portugal, and that each member state has its own implementing act.
(3) It does not invent Spanish or Portuguese requirements. It may note that MDR
    Art. 10(11) leaves language to the member state — but only if framed as a pointer,
    not quoted as verified text, since MDR is not carried by this skill.

FAIL if any MPDG section is applied as an obligation here. FAIL if it states a specific
Spanish or Portuguese language rule as fact.
