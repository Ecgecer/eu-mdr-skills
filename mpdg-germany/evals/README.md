## The two that earn most, both verified in the transcripts

### `doc-english-sufficient` — +1.00

A Dutch manufacturer asks whether the EU Declaration of Conformity must be translated
into German for the German market. It must not: **MPDG § 8(1)** permits it "in deutscher
**oder** in englischer Sprache".

With no skill, in 3 of 3 runs:

> "Short answer: yes — translate it into German." … citing **MDR Article 19(4)** as the
> controlling provision.

Art. 19(4) is real and says the DoC "shall be translated into an official Union language
or languages required by the Member State(s)". The baseline reached for it correctly and
then never checked what Germany actually requires — which is German **or** English.

This is the trap in its purest form: a real EU provision, quoted accurately, applied one
step past where it settles the question. Acting on it costs a translation nobody needed.

### `outside-carried-sections` — +1.00

A clinical-investigation question, which MPDG governs but this skill does not carry. With
no skill, in 3 of 3 runs, the baseline answered anyway — while saying it could not verify:

> "I tried to verify this against the current consolidated MPDG text [...] but web access
> is disabled in this session. So the below is from knowledge. I've marked the items where
> you should confirm the exact § number."

It knew it could not check and produced § numbers and deadlines regardless. The skill
declines and says which sections it holds.
